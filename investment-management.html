Process

All investment should be made considering only one factor, when is the money needed.

— Warren Buffett

In order to sell products in one meeting over the past 60 years, our industry has created many false narratives around investing, such as investing based upon your age and your risk tolerance. Endowments, corporations, banks, or the wealthiest families do not utilize age and risk tolerance as an investment criteria.

We follow a detailed approach to understand the exact time horizon of the goal at hand. We will then personalize your portfolio using low-risk investments only for money that is needed in the next four to five years, then using long-term investments such as equities for goals that are over five years. Bucketing money into short-term, mid-term, and long-term has proven to be far superior in countless studies by Nobel Prize-winning economists, as opposed to investing based upon risk tolerance.
