Fiduciary vs. Broker

What is a Fiduciary?

A fiduciary is a person or organization that acts on behalf of another person or persons, putting their clients’ interests ahead of their own, with a duty to preserve good faith and trust.

Financial Advisors vs. Fiduciaries

Not all financial advisors are fiduciaries. As a matter of fact, many aren’t. Brokers, also known as registered representatives, are only held to a suitability standard, which requires that the broker make recommendations that are suitable based on a client’s personal situation, but the standard does not require the advice to be in the client’s best interest. Therefore, some brokers may act for their own benefit.
