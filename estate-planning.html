Setting Up the Correct Estate Documents

It takes a lifetime to build a comfortable nest egg for retirement; however, many have not fully evaluated exactly what would happen in the event of a premature death. With a couple of conversations, a few swipes of the pen, and a few sentences of an estate plan, all of your wishes and proper protections for your loved ones can be achieved.

Protecting What You’ve Built

A well-structured estate plan doesn’t just transfer assets — it protects them. We work with you and your attorney to ensure your documents reflect your current family situation, address potential tax exposure, and account for life events that could affect your estate down the road.

Managing Unknown Risk

We find that many of our clients, when we first meet them, are unknowingly putting their children and loved ones at risk by leaving assets to them outright without realizing it. We walk through all the important considerations as they relate to each client’s specific family situation.

Eliminating Gaps in Your Plan

From improper beneficiary designations to outdated titling on accounts, small oversights can have large consequences. Our team conducts a thorough review to identify and close these gaps, so your estate plan does exactly what you intend — no surprises for the people you care about most.

Taking Care of Everything

We conduct a state alignment across all of the client’s assets and financial accounts, meaning once the proper estate planning documents are in place we directly help the client get account titling correct and proper beneficiaries updated on all financial accounts and assets.

Comprehensive Estate Planning Services

Personalized Estate Planning

Our team takes a personalized approach to craft tailored strategies that align with your unique financial goals, family dynamics, and legacy aspirations. Each client’s situation is distinct, and we are committed to creating solutions that address your specific needs.

Will and Trust Formation

We guide you through the intricacies of will and trust formation, helping you articulate your wishes clearly. Whether establishing a revocable living trust, irrevocable trust, or drafting a comprehensive will, our experts ensure your intentions are legally sound and seamlessly executed.

Probate Avoidance

Our team crafts strategies ensuring your estate passes smoothly to your beneficiaries while avoiding costly and time-consuming probate proceedings — preserving more of your wealth for the people and causes you care about.

Tax-Efficient Estate Planning

We help you structure your estate to minimize tax exposure for your beneficiaries. Our advisors work with your attorney to implement strategies that reduce estate taxes and preserve as much of your legacy as possible.

Business Succession Planning

We help business owners prepare for the eventual transition of their business — whether through sale, family transfer, or a buy-sell agreement — ensuring continuity and protecting the value you have built over a lifetime.

Charitable Giving Strategies

Whether establishing a charitable foundation or maximizing deductions through strategic donations, we guide you in making a positive impact while optimizing your estate’s tax position for future generations.

Family Wealth Education

We help families prepare the next generation to steward inherited wealth responsibly. Through thoughtful conversations and planning, we ensure your legacy is not only transferred but understood and preserved by those who receive it.

Power of Attorney & Healthcare Directives

Beyond financial assets, a complete estate plan addresses who speaks for you if you cannot. We ensure you have the right durable power of attorney, healthcare proxies, and living will documents in place to protect you and your family in any circumstance.
